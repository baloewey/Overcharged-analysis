INTERPRETATION (tentative: 1 seed per k, 4000 time units; no error bars yet)
  - H2 (effect fades when ρ·ℓ_a < 1): NOT supported at R/ℓ_a = 1.5. The bulk stays at net +1 down to
    ρ = 0.02 (ρ·ℓ_a = 0.43). The earlier apparent trend came from the edge/bulk cutoff.
  - H4 (bulk +1 comes with circulation): consistent. A strong steady vortex and a bulk of +1 co-occur in
    every run, but there's no contrast here (both "on" everywhere). Testing H4 needs the vortex to switch
    off, i.e. variation of R/ℓ_a (the main sweep), not of ρ at fixed R.
  - Method: a fixed edge/bulk cutoff isn't safe across k. Define the division per run from the data
    (the minimum of the radial +½ density, or where Q(r) plateaus at 1) and report the sensitivity.
    The d=2.5 default in circulation.py is too small for large cavities.
