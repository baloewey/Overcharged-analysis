FINDINGS
 - Compressibility is large at every activity used: density fluctuations 35% (act 0.05) to 120% (0.09) above
   Poisson; rms flow 0.66–1.0 c_s; the fastest 1% of cells are supersonic at all activities.
 - No threshold: both quantities grow smoothly with activity. The ℓ_a cutoff at 0.08 is not independently
   justified by a jump in density fluctuations.
 - Coincidence: Ma_rms (isothermal) reaches 1.00 exactly at act 0.085, where ℓ_a(ζ) flattens. Depends on the
   choice of c_s → suggestive only.
 - Caveats: one seed; short runs (the lowest activities may not be fully stationary: the ℓ_a sweep needed
   ~4000 steps at 0.05).

CONSEQUENCE
 - Quote as: "ℓ_a consistent with ζ^(−1/2) up to ζ = 0.08 (α = 0.485 ± 0.031); deviations above coincide
   with the rms flow approaching the sound speed." The cavity results are normalised by the measured
   ℓ_a(0.07) = 22.2 ± 0.3, so the exponent doesn't enter them. For other activities (H3), measure ℓ_a
   directly instead of extrapolating the power law.
