========================================================================
Written 2026-09-28 18:16 by active_length.py (runs/activeLength/L100/active_length.csv)

SETUP
  Box 100 x 100, periodic (domainWalls 1), 2D, dens 20 (200,000 particles), collOp 21, lc 1,
  other LC parameters default (mfpot 10, rfc 0.01, tumble 2). 30,000 steps (dt 0.1 -> t = 3000),
  warmUp 0 (ordered start; the transient is removed in the analysis). One seed per activity (1001-1009).
  defectsOut every 20 steps (1500 frames). Solver tracker (pout.c topoChargeAndDefectsOut).

METHOD
  N(t) = number of tracked defects per frame. Transient cut automatically: the start time that
  maximises the number of independent samples (Chodera 2016). <N> over the stationary part;
  error = std / sqrt(n_eff), n_eff = frames / (2 tau_int), tau_int by Sokal windowing; block-average
  (10 blocks) error as a cross-check.  l_a = sqrt(A / <N>), A = (L-2)^2 = 98^2 (the tracker skips
  the outermost cell layer); dl/l = (1/2) dN/N.  drift = (2nd-half mean - 1st-half mean) in sigma.

CAVEATS
  1. Box/l_a = 3.6-5 and only 13-24 defects per frame: finite-size effects possible, most at low
     activity. Check running: 200 x 200 at act 0.05, 0.07, 0.09 (runs/activeLength/L200, ~04:00).
  2. Stationarity: |drift| ~ 2 at act 0.050, 0.070, 0.075 -> slow fluctuations; one seed per
     activity. Low activity (0.050) has the longest transient (t_start ~ 400) and only n_eff ~ 19.
  3. Single seed per activity: seed-to-seed scatter not measured (the fit's chi2 hints at it).
  4. The defect tracker is the solver's legacy one (see TODO.md); results should be rechecked
     after the tracker rewrite.
