RESULT
  - No significant finite-size effect: differences are 1.1–1.6 sigma, with no consistent sign
    (relative differences -4.8%, +4.0%, +2.3%). Box/l_a goes from ~4-5 (L=100) to ~8-10 (L=200).
  - The flattening of l_a(act) seen at L=100 is not a finite-size artefact: at L=200,
    alpha from act 0.05 -> 0.09 = 0.40 ± 0.02 (L=100 full fit: 0.38 ± 0.04). alpha = 1/2 is excluded.
  - Reference for the cavity work: l_a(0.07) = 22.21 ± 0.26 (L=200, preferred: larger box, more defects)
    [error-weighted mean of both boxes: 22.01 ± 0.23]. Previous value 21.36 ± 0.47 (L=100) is ~4% lower;
    this shifts R/l_a and rho*l_a by ~4% but changes no conclusion so far (R-scan: R/l_a = 1.43 instead of 1.49).

CAVEATS
  - One seed per box and activity; seed-to-seed scatter not measured directly.
  - The transient at L=200 is long for act 0.05 and 0.09 (t_start ~ 620 of 2500): n_eff 41 and 85.
  - |drift| = 1.85 at act 0.09 (borderline). Legacy defect tracker (see TODO.md).
  - Charge-neutral frames: only 47-59% at L=200 (vs 62-78% at L=100) — more defects, more edge-strip
    losses, as predicted. The neutral filter is not used for l_a.
