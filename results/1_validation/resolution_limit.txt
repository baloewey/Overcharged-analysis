FINDINGS
 - The limit depends on the imposed defect spacing 1/rho, not on R (same pattern at R ≈ 8, 12, 16).
 - Passive runs fail exactly like active ones → it's the anchoring method (cell-scale resolution of a
   director turning by pi*rho rad per cell), not activity and not only the tracker. Alignment degrades first.
 - rho ≤ 0.10: clean. rho = 0.15: good (alignment ~0.9, audit ≥ 0.92). rho = 0.20: degraded (alignment ~0.78,
   up to 20% of frames missing imposed defects). rho ≥ 0.25: fails (audit ≤ 30%, anchoring ≤ 0.67).
 - Physically meaningful range: rho ≲ 0.15 (spacing ≳ 6–7 cells); 0.20 only with caution.
 - Q(R−1) on a circle 1 cell inside the wall is unreliable in small cavities (it passes through the layer);
   the audit is the robust check.
