Rows: 4 MISMATCH, 2 UNSOURCED, 13 AMBIGUOUS, 28 OK. Every number in the act_robust, lambda_crowd/lambda_ellipse, shear-scan, mirror-summary and mirror-table sets traces exactly to its output file. The wall-law Q_b/P values recompute to 0.01298/0.01275/0.01260 against law medians of 0.0123.

Most important problems:
1. **SI S12 crowding sentence.** "1.98–2.06" for the full −½ gain is a selected range: at R_eq=1.5 it is 1.81–2.04. "0.1–0.2 for the sparsest walls" contradicts the SI's own table (0.27).
2. **Theory report open question (iv).** The −½ tilt is about half the naive prediction (≈10° vs ≈20°), not a third.
3. **ζ=3 continuum result is out of date** in the theory report Sec. 5.1 and the tilt report. var_act_3 already shows a laminar disk with an ordered spiral, so the crowding drop arises without turbulence. The "being checked" clause and its use as support for the turbulent-bulk hypothesis need rewriting (RESEARCH 13:58).
4. **Unsourced.** The active d√s fit (24+59 d√s, 2.9° rms) and the exit rates 0.068/0.028 have no output file. The exit rates recompute correctly, but they are pooled over aspect 1.4–2, not aspect 2 as the PRL sentence implies.
5. **Claims vs scope.** "Adds two −½" holds only for crowded walls at R_eq≥1.5. "Main results hold at ζ 0.06–0.08" rests on one circle and one ellipse, and "within 5%" is borderline. "Whatever the flow alignment" rests on one 2-seed ellipse set. The "tilted by arccos(1/λ)" experimental prediction holds only for sparse walls. The −½ traffic numbers mix R_eq=1 with R_eq≥1.5. "End by annihilation" is not the majority outcome at R_eq=2.

No contradiction was found between the PRL v1, PRL v2, SI and theory report for the same number. The superseded wordings checked ("up to ten excess +½", "turbulent regime" in the PRL, "fewer in larger circles") were removed or are correct at fixed k.
