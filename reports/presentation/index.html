Overcharged active cavities

Why an active nematic puts charge +1 into the bulk, what sets the tilt, and how elongated cavities leak

Benjamin Loewe (with Claude) · NIAMH-MPCD + continuum Q-tensor checks

The puzzle

Anchoring winding k fixes the charge, not where it sits

  • Passive cavity: all 2k +½ defects at the wall
  • Active cavity: 2k−2 pinned at the wall, +1 in the bulk, single coherent vortex, wall defects tilted ≈ 62°
Circles, k = 2…10, R/ℓa sweep, 3 seeds (10 running on the server). Working activity ζ = 0.07.

1 · Geometry

2k−2 homeotropic sites, tilt locked to position

  • A wall +½ sits untilted only at one of the 2k−2 homeotropic points; elsewhere tilt and registry are one variable (a zero mode of the layer)
  • Splay ≠ bend pins the commensurate ring of 2k−2; a ring of 2k is frustrated

2 · Ejection

Coherent flow tilts the layer; a ring of 2k cannot follow

  • Common tilt costs a 2k ring 200–500 kBT vs 155–200 to eject two defects
  • A passive cavity under an imposed vortex ejects too; the continuum active Q-tensor model self-organises into the same state

3 · Hydrodynamics

The bulk drives the vortex; the layer brakes it

Seen from the bulk, the 2k−2 layer acts as a planar winding-1 wall; bulk +1 is the charge it encloses. Onset R/ℓa ≈ 0.4–0.6.

4 · The tilt angle

Flow alignment in the no-slip shear

τ = arccos(1/λ)

  • Flat-wall theory: zero mode + Leslie angle + factor 2 for a +½
  • Shear only in the wall band gives the same tilt; no shear there (slip) gives 96–101°
  • Active continuum: 61.7–62.1° vs MPCD ≈ 62°

5 · Shapes

One transition at aspect ≈ 1.3, ellipses = ovals

  • Coherence, circulation and the single vortex collapse together; scale-free
  • Leak is escape-driven and non-cascading; the continuum model reproduces the transition
  • Near-wall activity alone keeps the full layer even at aspect 2; bulk activity breaks it

6 · Escape dynamics

Elasticity does not let go

  • ≈ 1640 depleting escapes: restoring force −11 to −14 kBT/cell up to and through departure
  • Untilting first: cos τ rises over ≈ 40 t.u. while residents stay flat

6 · Escape dynamics

The escaper rides its own jet

  • Inward flow is local to the defect; the 4–8 cell ring stays flat
  • Excess flow at the core = the +½ own jet turned inward: 0.32 · cos τ

6 · Escape dynamics

The wall blocks the inward part of the jet

inward jet = 0.40 · cos τ · g(d),   g = 0.33 · 0.58 · 0.86 · 1 at depth 1–2 · 2–3 · 3–4 · ≥ 4 cells — no-penetration turns the jet along the wall

6 · Escape dynamics

The wall erases one vortex lobe

  • Wall-side lobe absent at depth 1–3; recovers over 3–6 cells (same scale as g)
  • Escapers' lobe recovers before they move: untilting swings it off the wall

Normal mobility μn ≈ 0.0016 ± 0.0004: −12 kBT/cell is only −0.02 cells/t.u. of drift. Untilting raises the inward projection; each cell of depth lets more jet through → runaway.

Untilting is driven, not rotational diffusion: a Langevin model with the residents' drift reproduces their excursions but not the escapers' 40 t.u. ramp.

7 · What sets the steady state

The tilt gates the traffic, not the population

  • λ test (λ 2 → 4): escapes drop ×10, as predicted — but the same gate slows refills too (exits ×6, entries ×3.7), so the wall count barely moves (7.1 → 7.65)
  • Not an elastic minimum: refilling a gap would lower the Frank energy by 80–100 kBT (ellipses and ovals)
  • Unique: seeded starts with 4, 7, 10 wall defects all converge to 7.2 ± 0.1 (circles to 10)
  • Self-limited by crowding: a sparser layer holds its defects more strongly
A dynamical balance of escapes and refills, far above the elastic optimum

7 · A wall law

Bulk charge per unit wall length

  • Saturates by aspect ≈ 1.5, size-independent: Qb ≈ c(q) · P  (∝ √area at fixed winding density q = k/P)
  • Circles are the exception: exactly +1 at every size

7 · The flat limit

Periodic channels with winding anchoring

  • Arclength rule → θ = θ0 ± 2πqx on the two walls; charge 2qL per period (new solver option)
  • Same wall law: 39–46% of the charge in the bulk at q = 0.031, any width 20–60 (aspect-2 ellipse: 40%)
  • Released +½ hover 5–18 cells off the wall; mobility separates them from the slow layer
  • Possible follow-ups: vortex rows/dancing with winding; mixed windings as a "topological capacitor"

Summary

The story in four lines

1. Anchoring winding → 2k−2 sites; coherent active flow tilts the layer and ejects two defects: bulk +1.
2. The tilt is flow alignment in the no-slip shear: τ = arccos(1/λ), confirmed for λ = 2–6.
3. Wall defects are protected by tilt and by the wall (jet turned along it, one lobe erased); untilting + jet → runaway escape.
4. Elongated cavities leak to a unique dynamical steady state that follows a wall law, Qb ≈ c(q)·P.
